Thermal trail volumetric fire references (reviewed 2026-10-25)

Primary algorithm reference:
https://github.com/mattatz/THREE.Fire
https://github.com/mattatz/THREE.Fire/blob/master/FireShader.js
The samplerFire approach uses upward-moving 3D turbulence to warp the height
coordinate of a flame profile. PM17N_ThermalTrail applies this idea to a long
ground trail with its own analytic profile, value noise, world-space continuity,
ray-box intersection and step-length-correct emission/absorption integration.
No Fire.png asset, three.js runtime or simplex-noise implementation is bundled.

Additional implementation reviewed for suitability:
https://github.com/Scrawk/GPU-GEMS-3D-Fluid-Simulation
This simulates a 3D fluid grid and ray traces it inside a cube. The short-lived
trail uses procedural density instead of introducing a fluid simulation grid.
These are established reference examples, not a claim of current maintenance
or drop-in Waylith compatibility.
